Formula reference

Everything the questions lean on ▾

Gaussian structure

\(B_t\sim N(0,t)\), \(\operatorname{cov}(B_s,B_t)=s\wedge t\).

Scaling \(B_{cs}\overset{d}{=}\sqrt{c}\,B_s\); increments over disjoint intervals are independent, so their covariance is the overlap length.

Moments: \(E[B_t^{2n}]=(2n-1)!!\,t^{n}\) — so \(E[B_t^2]=t,\ E[B_t^4]=3t^2,\ E[B_t^6]=15t^3\); odd moments \(=0\).

Conditioning

Split \(B_t=B_s+\Delta\), \(\Delta\sim N(0,t-s)\perp\mathcal F_s\).

\(E[B_t\mid\mathcal F_s]=B_s\), \(E[B_t^2\mid\mathcal F_s]=B_s^2+(t-s)\).

Backward: \(E[B_s\mid B_t]=\tfrac st B_t\), \(\operatorname{var}(B_s\mid B_t)=\tfrac{s(t-s)}{t}\).

Itô integral

Isometry: \(E\big[(\int_0^tH\,dB)^2\big]=\int_0^tE[H_s^2]\,ds\).

Polarisation: \(E\big[\int H\,dB\int K\,dB\big]=\int_0^tE[H_sK_s]\,ds\).

Deterministic \(f\): \(\int_0^t f\,dB\sim N\!\big(0,\int_0^tf^2\big)\) (Wiener integral, Gaussian).

Itô formula

\(df(B_t)=f'(B_t)\,dB_t+\tfrac12 f''(B_t)\,dt\).

\(\int_0^t B\,dB=\tfrac12(B_t^2-t)\); \(d(X_tY_t)=X\,dY+Y\,dX+d\langle X,Y\rangle\).

Exp. martingale \(e^{\theta B_t-\theta^2t/2}\); for \(X=B+\mu t\) it is a martingale when \(\theta=-2\mu\).

Quadratic variation & bracket

\(\langle B,B\rangle_t=t\); \(\langle\int H\,dB\rangle_t=\int_0^tH_s^2\,ds\); \(\langle\int H\,dB,\int K\,dB\rangle_t=\int_0^tH_sK_s\,ds\).

Finite variation \(A\): \(\langle A,A\rangle=0\), \(\langle A,M\rangle=0\). Independent BMs: \(\langle B^{(1)},B^{(2)}\rangle=0\).

Semimartingale \(X=M+A\): \(\langle X,X\rangle=\langle M,M\rangle\).

Stopping & paths

\(T\) stopping time: \(\{T\le t\}\in\mathcal F_t\). Two-valued \(a\mathbf1_A+b\mathbf1_{A^c}\) with \(A\in\mathcal F_u\): need \(u\le\min(a,b)\).

Reflection: \(P(\max_{s\le t}B_s\ge a)=2P(B_t\ge a)\). Gambler's ruin (barriers \(-a,b\)): \(P(\text{hit }b\text{ first})=\tfrac{a}{a+b}\).

Brownian paths: infinite variation, nowhere differentiable; QV over \([0,t]\) equals \(t\).
